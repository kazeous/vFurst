---

Use this checklist when preparing a new convention.

1. Create the organization and invite admins. Admins sign in with their Reyuu Games account; attendees create their accounts on the event portal.
2. Complete Stripe Connect onboarding.
3. Configure SMTP or SES sender settings.
4. Add a custom domain and publish CNAME/TXT DNS records.
5. Create events, registration tiers, hotel blocks, store products, volunteer departments, and vendor booth configuration.
6. Create API keys and webhook endpoints for integrations.
7. Run fee reconciliation before launch and after high-volume sales periods.

## Registration form and policies

Open an event's settings and choose **Registration form** to configure emergency contacts and custom questions.
Choose whether contacts are off, optional, or required, and whether to ask for one or two contacts.
Use **Policies** in event settings to publish the code of conduct, refund policy, and other required documents.
Each publication creates an immutable version; attendees must agree to every current policy when registering.
Cashier walk-ins and staff transfers require staff to attest that the attendee agreed to the current policies.
Existing registrations keep their recorded answers and agreements; new publications do not retroactively change them.
